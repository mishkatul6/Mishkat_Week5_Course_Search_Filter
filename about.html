ABOUT US

About YSSE

Youth School for Social Entrepreneurs helps young people learn practical skills and turn ideas into action for their communities.

Our mission

To give young learners accessible, practical education and the confidence to use their skills for social good.

Our vision

A generation of young people who learn, lead and build solutions to the challenges around them.

What we value

Learning by doing

Every course includes practice, not just theory.

Inclusion

Open to learners of different backgrounds and levels.

Community

We learn better together and support each other.

Impact

Skills should help people and communities.

What we offer

  • Beginner to advanced courses in web, design, marketing, programming and data
  • Workshops, talks and community events
  • A simple online enrollment process
